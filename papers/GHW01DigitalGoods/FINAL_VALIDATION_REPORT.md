## 6. Additional Assumptions Beyond Paper

Exactly three source/domain conventions are documented as additional
assumptions. None is a status caveat.

1. `GHW01-THEOREM93-SET-OF-BIDS-ERASED-MULTISET-01` interprets the paper's
   set of other bids as an erased multiset. It preserves duplicate bid values
   while forgetting presentation order and bidder labels.
2. `GHW01-WEIGHTED-PAIRING-PROBABILITY-DOMAIN-01` makes the weighted-pairing
   probability domain explicit. The paper-facing interface visibly assumes
   `forall i, 1 <= bids i` and `forall i, exists j, j != i`; Lean then derives
   a positive `T - bids i` denominator and constructs the probability profile
   internally.
3. `GHW01-RANDOMIZED-AUCTION-PMF-DOMAIN-01` interprets the source's unrestricted
   probability distributions on auction outcomes as Lean `PMF`s, hence as
   countably supported laws. Continuous outcome laws allowed by the literal
   source wording are not claimed.

to paper statements, not eight additional assumptions. The visible `h >= 2`

targets; they are source corrections rather than hidden premises. Theorem 8.2
is the user-approved finite randomized-offer target: its finite support is an
explicit added restriction of that target, not wording attributed to the
journal theorem. The restriction and the three conventions are not status
caveats. The fixed excess-rejection priority is not an additional assumption:
