## 6. Additional Assumptions Beyond Paper

The bounded-density routes use the visible literal-PDF convention
`PRPKG24-BOUNDED-PDF-MEASURE-01`. The all-coordinate fixed-k gamma-share
routes use `PRPKG24-POSITIVE-PREFERENCE-SUPPORT-01`: the source allows zero
type mass, so strict positive PMF support is recorded as an additional model
condition for Theorem 1(ii)--(iv) and Corollary 1, not as archival source
credit. Theorem 1(v)'s nonnegative common mean and the finite Bernoulli
nondegeneracy domains are likewise explicit.

## 7. Proof-Strategy Deviations

For Proposition 2, the displayed relaxed optimizer misses a finite type-count
shift. The corrected optimizer yields the finite coefficient `(2T+1)/N`,
rather than the printed sharp coefficient `(T+1)/N`; the asymptotic
square-root-share conclusion is unchanged. Theorem 2 uses the independent
rank-varying Bernoulli law required by the displayed rank-dependent marginal
schedule, rather than the internally inconsistent iid wording. Definitions 1
and 2 derive their normalizer nonzeroness from the finite PMF rather than
taking it as a caller-supplied premise.

## 8. Proof Tricks Worth Reusing

The normalizer repair obtains a positive PMF atom, proves its real gamma power
positive, and bounds the finite sum below by that summand. This avoids a
hidden nonzero-denominator assumption while preserving the source formula.

## 9. Generalizations, Conjectures, and Extensions

A supportwise treatment of zero-PMF coordinates could generalize the
all-coordinate share results, but it is not represented as an already-proved
extension. Any such extension should first specify whether coordinates with
zero selection probability are excluded or assigned a separate convention.
