then proves the source-facing conclusions from them. It does not accept an
outer-law, payoff, or conclusion-bearing certificate as a theorem premise.
For each named result, the top-level `PaperInterface.lean` statement carries
the full material conclusion; internal proof decomposition is not used to
weaken the human-facing claim.

Appendix C Lemma 1 states that Laplace noise is globally strictly well ordered.
That strict condition is false: separated ordered pairs yield equality. The
formal proof uses the globally valid weak inequality and proves strictness on
the explicit overlap region. No named main-text result depends on the false
global strict form.
