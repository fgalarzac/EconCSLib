## 10. Mathematical Typos or Other Fixes Suggested in the Source Paper

Theorem 8 defines `psi_k(i)` as the suffix sum of the displayed geometric dual
witness but prints exponent `k-i+1`. Direct summation gives exponent `k-i`.
The simple proof repeats the same shift when it calls `(k-i+1)/k` precisely the
unspent fraction after exact spend `i/k`; the coefficient is `(k-i)/k`. Lean
proves both corrected general identities. At `k=2,i=1`, the suffix sum is `1/2`
rather than `3/4`, and the unspent fraction is `1/2` rather than `1`. This one
finite off-by-one defect does not alter the `k -> infinity` theorem.

Appendix A says the tail bidders are exhausted after phase 2, all phase-3
queries are discarded, and the algorithm earns `0.62N`. There are two distinct
local defects. First, the printed zero-phase-3 contributions give
`0.4N + 0.2N + 0 = 0.6N`, not `0.62N`. Second, the actual fluid phase-1 spend is
`log(9/5)` and phase 2 leaves positive per-tail residual
`3/5-log(9/5)`. Serving it in phase 3 yields true normalized revenue
`9/10-(1/2)log(9/5)`. Lean proves the corrected state trajectory, online value,
offline optimum one, and strict comparison with `1-1/e`.
