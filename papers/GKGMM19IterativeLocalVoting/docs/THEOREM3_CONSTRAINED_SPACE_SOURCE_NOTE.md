# Theorem 3 Constrained-Space Clarification

## Source claim

Theorem 3 identifies a stationary limit of the iterative local-voting process
by the vanishing of its aggregate direction. The paper states that result in
the full ambient decision space.

## Precise reading

For a general constrained decision space, a projected limit supports the
following alternative: either the aggregate direction is zero, or it has no
feasible aggregate direction at that point. The second branch is a boundary
condition created by the constraint set, not a different preference model.

## Effect on the paper result

In the paper's full-space setting every aggregate direction is feasible, so the
second branch is impossible and the stated zero-direction conclusion follows.
This is a clarification of the full-space scope of the theorem, not a caveat
about its advertised conclusion.
